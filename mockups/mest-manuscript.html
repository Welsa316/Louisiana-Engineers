Mission

Engineering a stronger community.

MEST brings together Muslim professionals and students in engineering, science, and technology to build meaningful connections, exchange knowledge, and create opportunities for personal and professional growth.

Through mentorship, collaboration, and community engagement, we aim to support the next generation of innovators while strengthening the relationships that help our community thrive.

03

What we do

Three commitments, one line of work.

Each is a practice rather than a slogan, and each describes what a member can expect.

  • 01 / ConnectFig. 02

    Connect

  • 02 / MentorFig. 03

    Mentor

    Connect emerging professionals with experienced members who can share knowledge, perspective, and guidance.

  • 03 / AdvanceFig. 04

    Advance

    Encourage professional development, technical curiosity, and collaborative opportunities.
